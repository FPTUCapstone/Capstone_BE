# UC-10 item 6: AI behavior learning curve

> Generated by `tools/benchmarks/weighted-gate --behavior-learning`; do not edit by hand.

- Run date: 2026-11-11. Endpoint: `http://127.0.0.1:8002/v1/rank-pois` (team AI service, bge-m3) through the production `TripMateAiPoiRankingProvider`.
- Shipped configuration, `Csp:Mode=WeightedUtility`, 8-hour day. Two data sets:
  - **Real fixture** `danang-real-1day`: 19 real Da Nang POIs. The acceptance criteria are judged here.
  - **Simulated city** `simulated-look-alike-city`: 54 stops in seven families of look-alike places (8 bridges, 8 museums, 8 pagodas, 8 markets, 8 beaches, 8 hikes, 6 parks), real place names, synthetic positions. As in production, only the 40 best-ranked candidates reach the solver.
- 12 simulated travelers × 5 trips, 1 day apart (consecutive days of one stay). Each traveler declares a broad interest (or none) and has a **hidden taste** the system never sees.
- After every trip the traveler removes the stops they hate (Dislike, itinerary edit, weight 1.0) and visits the rest (Like, navigation, weight 0.3). The ranking sees only those signals, through the production weights, 90-day decay, netting and count-based affinity. A fixture's fixed mandatory stop is not counted as a choice.
- **Removed-stop cooldown** (decision D8): a stop removed in the last 14 days is not suggested again. All simulated trips fall inside that window. Rows A1 and C1 turn it off to show each kind of learning on its own.
- **Hit rate**: share of optional stops the traveler truly likes. **Hate rate**: share they would remove. Mandatory stops are excluded.

## How to read it

- A and B cannot learn: every trip is the same day.
- A1 and C1 have no cooldown: A1 learns by counting (the same POI, or its whole category), C1 through the AI profile, which also moves POIs that resemble what was liked or removed.
- A2 and C add the cooldown: a removed stop does not come back for 14 days.
- **Stops removed** is the effort left to the travelers: every hated stop the ranking suggested had to be removed by hand.

What the two data sets show:

- **AI profile against counting, no cooldown** (C1 vs A1), last trip: real fixture hit 49 % / hate 20 % vs 41 % / 30 %; simulated city 28 % / 17 % vs 29 % / 26 %.
- **Shipped against rules + cooldown** (C vs A2), last trip: real fixture hit 54 % / hate 0 % vs 59 % / 0 %; simulated city 34 % / 1 % vs 34 % / 3 %.
- **Effort over 5 trips** (stops removed, C vs A2): real fixture 24 vs 23; simulated city 46 vs 55.
