# Staff resolution after bounded complaint negotiation

User approved implementation: final Traveler refusal after TO's second offer routes to AwaitingStaffReview, not a closed Declined complaint. No refund obligation exists until agreement or a binding Staff refund decision. Staff—not Admin by default—reviews evidence, negotiation history and contractual basis. Staff may request evidence from Traveler or TO, reject with reasons, or decide full/partial refund within verified paid/available cap. No extra acceptance of a Staff refund decision is required. Preserve operator/traveler negotiation limits and direct refund/payment safeguards.

Staff decisions require reason, contractual basis, current row version and operationId. Decisions/events are durable, actor/time stamped and idempotent; creation of a refund and final complaint decision must be atomic. Bind the resulting direct refund to complaint source. Reuse saved recipient snapshot and existing transfer/72h/dispute lifecycle. Do not fabricate legal contract wording or legal authority; published template/signatures remain unchanged.

Staff queue and detail: GET /api/v1/staff/post-trip-complaints, GET /{id}, POST /{id}/decisions and GET /{id}/evidence/{evidenceId}. List includes active review/evidence requests and final Staff-reviewed history. Party-specific views expose only own complaint, review outcome and requested evidence actions. Supplemental evidence uses private existing storage, PDF/JPG/PNG <=5MB/file, <=3 files/response, ownership/target-request checks, independent of the initial complaint's one-amendment limit. Keep original files and append history; never reopen monetary negotiation implicitly.

User approved evidence response SLA: 72 hours from the Staff request, measured by server UTC. Expiry returns the complaint to Staff review and records the elapsed request; it never rejects the complaint automatically. Allow own-party reconsideration requests after a Staff final decision only with new evidence and a reason; append a separate durable request/history entry without changing the decision, reopening negotiation, changing any refund obligation or creating duplicate money. Reconsideration adjudication/financial adjustment is a separate future workflow.

No automatic reopening/backfill of historical Declined complaints, real bank transfer, remote deployment or push authorized. Test existing DB only after verified backup and additive idempotent migration.

Completed-refund closure (user-approved correction): once the related complaint refund is completed (direct Completed, including automatic/Admin resolution, or legacy Processed), the complaint is read-only for both parties. Keep history and evidence downloadable; remove evidence/reconsideration actions and reject new submissions at the backend, including stale UI requests. Reconsideration intake remains available before completion; an idempotent replay of an already recorded submission is not a new submission. Match the complaint source/reference, not an unrelated refund on the same booking.
